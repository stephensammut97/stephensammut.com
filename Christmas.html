As part of the run up to Christmas, our team developed the home of Christmas campaign which was split into three phases. The first phase was the early bird phase which was for the shoppers who wanted to get their Christmas shopping done early. The second phase was the gifting phase which was for the shoppers who wanted to give gifts to their loved ones. The third phase was the final phase which was for the shoppers who wanted to complete their Christmas shopping before the holidays.

These initial concepts were what I developed to present to stakeholders with the proposed look and feel for the Christmas campaign landing page across the Freemans and Kaleidoscope websites. The designs explored how the campaign typography guidelines, photography and visual styling could come together to create a cohesive festive experience, while ensuring key commercial messaging remained prominent. This included giving the credit banner clear visibility within the page, recognising its importance in communicating a key customer proposition. The approved direction then provided a visual foundation for the wider Christmas campaign and onsite updates.

PHASE 2

The key feedback from stakeholders for Phase 2 was to make our credit options more prominent on the homepage, as this was an important proposition for customers during the festive period. The main change was introducing the credit message directly into the hero banner, ensuring it was immediately visible without taking away from the main campaign message. The landing page was designed with a clean, engaging layout, clear calls to action and intuitive navigation, making it easy for customers to explore the campaign and understand the available credit options. The final design balanced the promotional messaging with the credit proposition, creating a clear and seamless journey for customers.
